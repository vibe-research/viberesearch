# Open researcher feedback

Comment here, or under the researcher comments section of a report/experiment.
The agent keeps your wording and replies with the change, evidence, or remaining
disagreement. Link to a document revision/section when possible.

For inline feedback, use a heading such as `### C-001` and a separate line
`Review-status: open` so a fresh session can find it. Freeform comments here are
also welcome. “Addressed” means a requested edit is made; it need not mean the
scientific concern is settled.
